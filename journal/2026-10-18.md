## Why
- One file per section keeps merge conflicts down with four people writing in the same week.
- The lecturer said most ideas build on earlier work and we should say where ours came from, so Related Work spends most of its space on the papers we built on and what we do differently.
- We picked SHAP over permutation importance because permutation importance needs the true price, and a model running live doesn't have it yet. The same reason applies to Lundberg et al.'s monitoring example: it splits the model's loss across features, and loss needs the true outcome.
- The "size not order" point came out of the conclusion draft. Our results showed it all along (yesterday's price kept growing but tau didn't move), but writing it up made it clear it's one of our main findings, not a side note.
